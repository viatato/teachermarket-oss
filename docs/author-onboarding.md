# Author Onboarding

Use this checklist to onboard an author without implying that TeacherMarket sells materials or pays authors on their behalf.

## Before The First Upload

1. Explain that buyers contact authors directly in Telegram and arrange any transaction outside TeacherMarket OSS v1.
2. Share the deployment's [service rules](service-rules.md), support contact, moderation process, and complaint policy.
3. Confirm that the author has a Telegram username they are willing to use for buyer contact.
4. Ask the author to create a seller profile with a clear display name, short bio, and current contact username.
5. Explain the deployment's visibility model. A published material may require an eligible free-tier slot, an active author subscription, or an active one-time placement when those optional modules are enabled.

## Material Checklist

- The author owns the material or has permission to distribute it.
- Files contain no student or unrelated personal data.
- The title and description match the delivered material.
- The audience, level, language, format, and lesson use are clear.
- Preview images are readable and representative but do not expose the full private product file.
- The product file uses an allowed format and stays within the deployment's upload limit.

Suggested description template:

```text
Title:
Audience and level:
What is included:
How to use it in a lesson:
File format:
Estimated activity duration:
```

## First Publication

1. Upload the product file and previews.
2. Save the material as a draft and review every field.
3. Submit it for moderation.
4. Apply requested changes and resubmit when necessary.
5. After publication, confirm that the catalog card and previews are correct.
6. From a separate buyer account, confirm that **Contact author** opens the intended Telegram conversation and creates a contact request.
7. Show the author where to review contact requests, material status, reports, and visibility status.

Do not use real student data, private customer files, or live payment credentials during onboarding tests.
