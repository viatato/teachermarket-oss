# TeacherMarket Service Rules

This public-safe baseline is for TeacherMarket self-hosters. Publish an adapted version before inviting authors or buyers, and provide a private support channel for questions and appeals.

## Platform Role

TeacherMarket is a moderated catalog that connects buyers with independent authors through Telegram contact requests. OSS v1 does not accept payment for individual materials, deliver purchased files to buyers, guarantee off-platform transactions, or pay authors out.

Author subscriptions and optional one-time placements pay for catalog visibility and service access. They do not buy a material and do not guarantee sales, views, or contacts.

## Author Rules

Authors must:

- upload only original materials or materials they are allowed to distribute;
- keep student and other personal data out of files, previews, titles, and descriptions;
- describe the material, format, audience, and intended use truthfully;
- use previews that represent the actual material without exposing the complete private file;
- keep their Telegram contact details current and communicate respectfully;
- follow the host community's copyright, safety, and content rules.

Authors remain responsible for their materials and any agreement made directly with a buyer.

## Buyer Rules

Buyers must:

- use contact and report tools in good faith;
- avoid posting personal data, payment details, or private files in report reasons;
- agree delivery and payment terms directly with the author;
- report suspected copyright, privacy, safety, fraud, or material-description issues through the host's private support route.

## Moderation

Admins may approve, reject, request changes, hide, or restore a material. A report is a request for review, not proof of a violation. Decisions should disclose only the minimum information needed and must not reveal a reporter's identity to the author.

Use the [material complaint policy](complaint-policy.md) for the review workflow. Where practical, offer authors a private appeal route.

## Deployment Responsibilities

Self-hosters must define applicable terms, response targets, retention periods, prohibited content, support contacts, and payment/refund terms for their jurisdiction. TeacherMarket's OSS documentation is an operational baseline, not legal advice.
