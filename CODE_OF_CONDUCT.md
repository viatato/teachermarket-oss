# Code Of Conduct

TeacherMarket is for teachers, education creators, and contributors building useful community infrastructure.

We expect participants to:

- be respectful and constructive;
- assume good intent while still naming problems clearly;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, and personal attacks;
- protect private teacher, student, buyer, and maintainer data;
- avoid posting copyrighted materials, credentials, or private files without permission.

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior harms the project or community.
